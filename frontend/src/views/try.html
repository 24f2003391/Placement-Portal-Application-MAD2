Platform Features

Designed for Students, Companies and Placement Administrators.

🎓 Students

  • Browse placement drives
  • Apply to eligible jobs
  • Track applications
  • View interview schedules
  • Accept or reject offers

🏢 Companies

  • Register organization
  • Create placement drives
  • Review applications
  • Schedule interviews
  • Send offer letters

👨‍💼 Placement Cell

  • Approve companies
  • Manage students
  • Monitor placement drives
  • Generate reports
  • Track placement statistics

About the Portal

This Placement Portal streamlines the campus recruitment process by enabling students to apply for placement opportunities, companies to recruit candidates efficiently, and placement administrators to oversee the complete recruitment lifecycle from company approval to final placements.
